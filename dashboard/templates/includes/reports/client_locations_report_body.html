Client Locations

Every client's postcode for a coach (or everyone), plotted on a map so you can see roughly where a coach is working. Shows the client's own postcode and, where set, their Therapy Location's postcode. If a coach has Territory Postcode Areas set on their Coach record, clients outside that coach's own area - or inside another coach's - are flagged in red, and that coach's assigned area is outlined on the map in their own colour using the real shape of those postcode areas/districts (free data, (c) Wikipedia contributors, CC BY-SA 3.0 - see dashboard/data/uk_postcode_areas/ATTRIBUTION.md), not an approximation - clients may sit inside or outside it depending on where they actually live. Falls back to a Voronoi-based approximation only for a postcode this free dataset doesn't cover (Northern Ireland or the Channel Islands/Isle of Man), or a coach with no Territory Postcode Areas set at all, in which case the outline is instead based on where their actual in-area clients happen to sit.
